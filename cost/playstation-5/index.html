How many hours of work is a PlayStation 5?

At $20 an hour before taxes, a PlayStation 5 costs 32.5 hours of work, or 4.1 workdays of 8 hours. Price: $649.99.

The PS5 got more expensive in 2026, not cheaper, which breaks the old habit of waiting a couple of years for a console to drop in price. That makes the hours it costs today worth knowing before you buy.

Before you buy it

Decide between the disc and Digital editions first. The $50 difference is real hours of work, but disc games can be bought used and resold, while digital games cannot.

Online multiplayer needs a PlayStation Plus subscription, and that is a yearly cost on top of the console. Put a year of it into the calculator as its own purchase.

If you only play a few big games a year, ask whether the hours of work match the hours of play. If they do, buy it without guilt.

Price: $649.99. US list price for the PS5 with a disc drive since April 2, 2026. The Digital Edition costs $599.99 and the PS5 Pro $899.99. Source: PlayStation Blog, checked October 30, 2026. Prices outside the US differ: use the calculator with your local price and currency.
