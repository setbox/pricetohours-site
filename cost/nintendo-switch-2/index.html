How many hours of work is a Nintendo Switch 2?

At $20 an hour before taxes, a Nintendo Switch 2 costs 25 hours of work, or 3.1 workdays of 8 hours. Price: $499.99.

The Switch 2 is a console people tend to buy once and use for years, often shared by a whole family. That makes it a good candidate for the hours test: the price is clear, and so is the question of how much you will really play.

Before you buy it

Count the hours you expect to play in the first year and compare them with the hours of work above. If you will play more hours than you worked to pay for it, most people would call that a fair trade.

The console is only the start. Games, a second controller, a case and the online subscription all come on top, and each one is more time at work. Add them up before you decide, not after.

The price went up $50 in September 2026. If you were waiting for a discount, that wait has already cost you. Holiday bundles that include a game can be the better deal in hours.

Price: $499.99. US list price for the standard console since September 1, 2026, when Nintendo raised it from $449.99. Source: PC Gamer, checked October 30, 2026. Prices outside the US differ: use the calculator with your local price and currency.
