How many hours of work is a year of Netflix?

At $20 an hour before taxes, a year of Netflix costs 12 hours of work, or 1.5 workdays of 8 hours. Price: $239.88.

Subscriptions feel cheap because you see the monthly price. Over a year they add up, and most people pay for more than one. Looking at a full year in hours makes the cost visible.

Before you renew

Check how many hours you actually watched last month. If the hours of work for a year are more than the hours you will watch, the plan with ads or a pause may make more sense.

Rotate services instead of stacking them. One streaming service at a time, switched every few months, can cut the yearly hours of work in half or more.

Do the same test for every subscription you have. Put each one in as a yearly price and add up the hours. The total is often surprising.

Price: $239.88. Twelve months of the US ad-free Standard plan at $19.99 a month, the price since the March 2026 increase. The plan with ads costs $8.99 a month and Premium $26.99. Source: TheWrap, checked October 30, 2026. Prices outside the US differ: use the calculator with your local price and currency.
