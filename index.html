How the math works

The calculator divides the price by your hourly pay. If you enter a salary, it first turns it into an hourly rate: a yearly salary is divided by 52 weeks, then by your workdays and hours. A monthly salary is multiplied by 12 before that, because months have different numbers of workdays.

The true cost goes one step further. Rent, food and bills get paid first, so only what is left is free to spend. Divide that by your hours and the free hourly rate is usually much lower than your pay. Every purchase costs more of your time than it seems.

hours = price ÷ hourly pay
hourly pay = yearly salary ÷ 52 ÷ days ÷ hours
free hourly rate = (take-home − bills) × 12 ÷ 52 ÷ days ÷ hours
