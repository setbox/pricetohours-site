$25 an hour is how much a year?

At 40 hours a week and 52 weeks a year, $25 an hour is $52,000 a year before taxes. That is $4,333.33 a month and $1,000 a week.

$25 an hour by period

Before taxes. Full time is 40 hours a week, part time 30 hours. A year is 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours at full time.

What $25 an hour means

$25 an hour crosses $50,000 a year at full time. At this level the difference between gross and take-home pay starts to matter more, because more of each extra dollar goes to taxes.

These numbers are gross pay. Federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare come out before the money reaches you, and how much depends on where you live and your situation. Gross or net: which pay to use explains which number to use when you compare prices.

Paid time off changes the math too. If your 2,080 hours include paid holidays and vacation, the yearly figure above is what you earn. If you are paid only for hours worked, subtract the weeks you take off.
