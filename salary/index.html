How the conversion works

A full-time year is usually counted as 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 hours. Multiply an hourly wage by 2,080 to get a yearly salary, or divide a salary by 2,080 to get the hourly rate. A monthly figure is the yearly figure divided by 12, not the weekly figure times four, because a month has a little over four weeks.

These are gross numbers. Income tax and payroll taxes come out before the money reaches you, so your take-home pay is lower. When you compare prices with your time, which number to use depends on the question you are asking.
